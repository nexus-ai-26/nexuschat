## Hackathon (UniPods Chatbot Hackathon)
- The hackathon runs from 18 September to 24 September 2026. The submission deadline is Thursday 24 September 2026.
- Teams can have up to 5 members and must include at least one woman.
- Members should come from different countries. Reported rule: up to 2 members from the same country are allowed.
- The submission must be a working chatbot, not just slides or a mock-up.
- The submission includes access to the source code/repository and short setup notes.
- The bot can be delivered through WhatsApp, Telegram or a web interface. No single platform is required.
- Bots are named with the team name followed by "BOT".
- Teams must inform Diane before deploying a bot. Bots must not be deployed randomly.
- The team declaration deadline was Thursday 17 September. To declare a team, the team lead emails the team name, members and their countries to unipods.regional@undp.org with the subject "UniPods Hackathon - Team Declaration". There is no separate declaration link. For late declarations, confirm with Diane whether a new team can still be registered.
- The 5-minute UN video-demo submission deadline (18 September, 2:00 PM CAT) has passed.

## Selection of the 50 projects
- The 50 projects are selected from teams that complete the MIT Universal AI course and stay up to date with the Wadhwani programme.
- These teams work with the Ethiopian AI Institute to develop a tailored workplan.
- In the last week of November 2026, the Institute selects the 50 strongest solutions for the Addis Ababa bootcamp. Each selected team sends one participant.
- Teams not selected are considered for the second bootcamp in February 2027.
